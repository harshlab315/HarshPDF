Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13 Sep 2026 | Email: viscosity315@gmail.com | Website: kaagazpro.co.in

At KaagazPro, your privacy is our priority. We built this site as a 100% offline, browser-based toolkit. That means your files are processed inside your own browser, not on our server.

1. Zero Storage Policy - We Do NOT Store Your Files

This is our core principle. All tools (Resize to KB, Compressor, JPG to PDF, PDF to JPG, Background Remover etc.) use client-side technology like HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Your photos, PDFs and documents never leave your device, are never uploaded to our server, never viewed and never stored by us. When you close the tab, data is cleared from browser memory.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your photos, Aadhaar, PAN, signature or any document images
  • We do NOT collect your name, phone number, address or personal details (except what you send via contact form)
  • We do NOT save compressed or converted files on any server
  • We do NOT have access to your files

3. What We Collect (Anonymous Only)

To keep KaagazPro fast and free, we use:

  • Google Analytics: Anonymous data like device type, browser type, pages visited, approximate location (city level). No personal files.
  • Contact Form: Only when you contact us via form at contact.html, we receive your name, email and message via FormSubmit to reply you. We don't share it.

4. Cookies & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads and keep all tools free. Google may use cookies and DoubleClick DART cookies to show relevant ads. You can:

  • Opt-out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com
  • Disable cookies from your browser settings

AdSense does NOT get access to your files, because files never leave your device.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please check their policies when you visit them.

6. Children's Privacy

KaagazPro is for general audience (students, job seekers, professionals). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Our tools work without login.

7. Data Security

Since we don't store your files, there is no risk of file leak from our server. Your files remain on your device. We use HTTPS to secure the connection between your browser and our website.

8. Your Consent & Contact

By using KaagazPro.co.in, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you have any question about privacy, you can contact us anytime and we reply within 24 hours.
