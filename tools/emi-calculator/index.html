    • ❓ EMI FAQs

    EMI kaise calculate hota hai?▼
    Formula: EMI = P x R x (1+R)^N / [(1+R)^N-1]. Jaha P=Loan, R=Monthly Interest, N=Months. Hamara calculator bank wala hi formula use karta hai.
    Total interest kya hai?▼
    Total Interest = (EMI x Months) - Loan Amount. Ye extra paisa hai jo aap bank ko byaaj ke roop me dete ho.
