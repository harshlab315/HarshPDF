GST FAQ - Simple Answers

?
What is GST?

GST is Goods and Services Tax. It's a single tax on goods and services, e.g. 18% on base price.

?
How is GST calculated?

Exclusive: GST = Amount × Rate / 100. Total = Amount + GST.
Inclusive: Original = Total × 100 / (100+Rate). GST = Total - Original.

?
What is CGST and SGST?

Total GST is divided into two halves for same-state sale. CGST = GST/2 goes to Central Govt. SGST = GST/2 goes to State Govt. For other state sale, IGST = full GST.

?
Example: ₹10,000 with 18%?

GST = 10000×18/100 = ₹1,800. CGST = ₹900, SGST = ₹900. Total = ₹11,800.

?
How to calculate inclusive price? (₹11,800 inclusive 18%)

Original = 11800×100/118 = ₹10,000. GST = 11800-10000 = ₹1,800.
