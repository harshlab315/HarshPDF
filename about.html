👨‍💻 Our Story

KaagazPro.co.in is a small initiative started from Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. We are not a big company, we are just a small team who loves solving real problems. We built this tool because we faced the same problem ourselves - forms rejecting photos due to size limits.

We believe privacy should not be a premium feature. That's why our tool works 100% offline. No tracking of your photos, no Aadhaar collection, nothing.
